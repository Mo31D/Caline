## High-level conclusions

### Preserve
- Caline kangaroo/script identity and `PASSION AU CHOCOLATE` logo asset.
- Verified Hamriyah Free Zone manufacturing story.
- Historically documented handmade Belgian-chocolate / bean-to-bar positioning, with careful wording.
- Evidence-backed product families such as assorted chocolates, dark chocolate, pralines/bonbons and gift presentation, subject to current catalogue confirmation.

### Do not migrate as source truth
- current prototype product names and prices;
- fake/demo LocalStorage store and checkout;
- unsupported statistics;
- template testimonials;
- unsupported promotions;
- stock cake/doughnut/wafer imagery;
- unrelated “Ghoco” template branding;
- current Riyadh “first branch Dec 2024 / Al Olaya” story.

## Important research warning

The word **Caline** is also used as a product name by unrelated chocolate companies, especially Patchi and Morozoff.

Do not import products from search results solely because they contain the name “Caline”.

Every product added to the rebuilt site must be linked specifically to Caline Factory / Caline FZE / a confirmed official regional Caline operation.

## Stage 10 implementation gate

No public-facing rebuild has been started yet.

Before implementation, choose:

### A. Corporate + catalogue first
Build the professional website, manufacturing/story pages and verified catalogue structure first; add ecommerce after authoritative SKU data is available.

### B. Full ecommerce
Requires authoritative current:
- SKU list;
- prices;
- weights/piece counts;
- ingredients/allergens;
- images;
- delivery rules;
- payment/platform decisions.
