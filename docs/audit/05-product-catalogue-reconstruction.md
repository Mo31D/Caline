# Caline Website Audit — Stage 5: Product & Catalogue Reconstruction

**Project:** Caline Chocolate  
**Repository:** `Mo31D/Caline`  
**Research date:** 2026-09-23  
**Purpose:** Reconstruct only the product families/SKUs that can be tied to Caline Factory or its historically documented regional boutiques.

---

# 1. Evidence rules used in this stage

Because “Caline” is also a product name used by unrelated chocolate companies, catalogue reconstruction needs stricter rules than normal web research.

## Confidence grades

### A — Strong / primary
Evidence from:
- Caline executive/company statement;
- Hamriyah Free Zone Authority;
- clearly branded first-party material tied to Caline Factory.

### B — Strong historical retail evidence
Evidence from:
- established media showing Caline-branded product/packaging;
- clearly Caline-branded historic imagery;
- an old Caline regional boutique/site linked independently to the business.

### C — Regional marketplace/directory evidence
Useful for proving an item was sold by a regional Caline listing, but not enough to make it a global/current factory SKU.

### D — Unsupported / reject
- only appears on the current 2025 prototype;
- comes from another company also using the word “Caline”;
- cannot be tied to Caline Factory.

# 2. Product families that are safe to associate with Caline

## 2.1 Handmade Belgian chocolates

**Confidence: A**

Gulf News published a direct statement from Abdullah Abdullatif Alarfaj, CEO of Caline Factories, saying that since 2016 Caline Chocolate factory specialises in manufacturing handmade Belgian chocolates.

This is the strongest product-positioning statement found.

**Use on new site:** YES, with appropriate historical/company wording.

Source:  
https://gulfnews.com/business/yes-we-did-it-1.1617278253127

---

## 2.2 Moulded and packaged chocolate consumer products

**Confidence: A**

Hamriyah Free Zone Authority documented students watching production at the Caline Chocolates factory from raw chocolate through moulding and packaging into different consumer products.

This proves that Caline is not merely a reseller/boutique; moulded chocolate production is part of the manufacturing operation.

**Use on new site:** YES.

Source:  
https://hfza.ae/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Issue-45.pdf

---

## 2.3 Assorted praline / bonbon gift boxes

**Confidence: B**

A 2019 article titled **“CALINE PASSION AU CHOCOLATE”** contains a clearly Caline-branded luxury presentation box with the kangaroo logo. The open box contains approximately twenty individually compartmented assorted chocolates/bonbons in:
- dark chocolate;
- milk chocolate;
- white chocolate;
- decorated/coloured pieces;
- several moulded shapes.

The visual is useful evidence for the type of product Caline historically presented as a premium gift assortment.

It is **not** sufficient to assign exact fillings, weight, price or current availability.

**Use on new site:** Product family YES after owner confirmation of current availability.  
**Exact SKU:** NO, not recovered.

Source:  
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/caline-passion-au-chocolate-zabeed-shah

---

## 2.4 Dark chocolate presentation / individually wrapped dark chocolates

**Confidence: B**

Time Out Riyadh’s historical luxury-chocolate coverage contains a clearly Caline-branded product photograph:
- white presentation packaging;
- Caline kangaroo/logo;
- visible wording **“DARK CHOCOLATE”**;
- individually wrapped dark-chocolate pieces/cubes carrying the Caline name.

This is strong historical evidence that dark chocolate was a genuine Caline retail product family in Riyadh.

Do not infer cocoa percentage, filling, net weight or current availability from the photo.

**Use on new site:** Category/family YES after current confirmation.

Source:  
https://www.timeoutriyadh.com/food-drink/buy-luxury-chocolate-in-riyadh

# 6. IMPORTANT false positive: Patchi “Caline”

This is the largest catalogue-research trap.

**Patchi** currently sells a product named:

> **Milk Chocolate Bar With Hazelnuts, Caline, No Added Sugar**

This is a Patchi product whose SKU/product name happens to contain **Caline**.

Patchi’s own site identifies it as a Patchi product and supplies its own SKU, ingredients and pricing.

It is **NOT evidence of a Caline Factory sugar-free product**.

Examples:
- https://patchi.com/uk/en/product/milk-chocolate-bar-caline-no-added-sugar-50g
- https://patchi.com/ksa-mecca/en/product/milk-chocolate-bar-caline-no-added-sugar-50g

### Rule for future work

Never import search results containing:
- “Patchi Caline”
- “Caline Milk Chocolate Bar with Hazelnuts”
- “Caline No Added Sugar”

unless there is separate evidence explicitly tying the item to **Caline Factory FZE**. Current results tie these products to Patchi, so they must be rejected.

# 10. What is still missing before a real ecommerce catalogue can be built

For each current product Caline actually wants to sell, we still need authoritative source data for:

1. exact commercial product name;
2. product image;
3. net weight / piece count;
4. flavours/fillings;
5. ingredients;
6. allergen information;
7. nutrition data if required;
8. packaging options;
9. retail price by market;
10. current availability;
11. SKU/internal code;
12. shelf life/storage;
13. delivery territory.

The old web traces are sufficient to reconstruct **product families and brand direction**, but not sufficient to invent a complete 2026 retail catalogue.

---

# 11. Recommended product architecture for the redesign

Until a current master catalogue is obtained, the website should be structured around evidence-backed categories rather than fictional SKUs:

- **Chocolate Collections**
  - Dark Chocolate
  - Milk Chocolate
  - White Chocolate
  - Assorted Pralines & Bonbons
  - Truffles
  - Gift Boxes

- **Manufacturing**
  - Handmade Belgian chocolate positioning
  - Bean-to-bar process
  - Hamriyah Free Zone production

- **Regional / Cafe products**
  - kept separate until ownership and current market availability are confirmed.

A “Sugar Free” category should **not** be created solely because the prototype already contains one. Current search results for “Caline sugar free” are heavily contaminated by Patchi’s unrelated product named Caline.

---

# 12. Stage 5 conclusion

The current prototype catalogue should be considered **invalid as a source of truth**.

The credible historical Caline product identity is built around:

- handmade Belgian chocolates;
- moulded chocolates;
- premium assorted bonbons/pralines;
- dark and white chocolate selections;
- decorated premium truffles;
- gift-box presentation.

Only two named/weighted products were recovered externally under a current regional Caline marketplace listing, both in Oman, and both remain market-specific pending owner confirmation.

This is enough to proceed with a credible brand/design reconstruction without inventing products.
