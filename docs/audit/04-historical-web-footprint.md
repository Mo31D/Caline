# Caline Website Audit — Stage 4: Old Websites & Historical Digital Footprint

**Project:** Caline Chocolate  
**Repository:** `Mo31D/Caline`  
**Research date:** 2026-09-23

## Objective

Identify Caline’s older domains, regional web stores and digital traces before the current `calinefactory.com` prototype, and establish which sources are useful for recovering the real product catalogue.

---

# Executive finding

The current domain is **not** the historical web home of the brand.

Evidence indicates:

- `calinefactory.com` was newly registered around **20 April 2025**, at the same time the current GitHub prototype was being built.
- A **Caline Riyadh** Shopify store existed under `calinechocolate-csa.com` by June 2021.
- A **Caline Oman** site existed under `calinechocolate-om.com` in 2021 and is tied by business-directory evidence to the Salalah shop.
- `calinechocolate-bh.com` appears as a Bahrain food e-commerce domain in a 2025 commerce dataset, but ownership is not yet independently strong enough to mark it fully verified.
- Historical retail footprints exist in Jumeirah/Dubai, Riyadh and Salalah/Oman, and there is evidence of a Khobar boutique.
- `calinechocolate.com` exists in historical domain/redirect datasets, but no reliable evidence found in this stage links that domain to Caline Chocolate FZE. It must **not** be assumed to be the old corporate site.

This suggests Caline’s historic digital presence was **fragmented by market**, rather than centred on the current `calinefactory.com` domain.

# 9. What we learned about the original web architecture

The evidence suggests that Caline may historically have operated **country-specific web stores/retail presences** rather than a single global e-commerce website.

The pattern is particularly visible in:

- Saudi: `calinechocolate-csa.com`
- Oman: `calinechocolate-om.com`
- Bahrain lead: `calinechocolate-bh.com`

This matters for the rebuild.

A new corporate site should avoid recreating fragmented country sites unless there is a genuine operational reason. A cleaner architecture would normally use one authoritative brand domain with regional catalogue/location data beneath it.

---

# 10. Archive retrieval limitation

Direct historical-page retrieval from the Internet Archive/CDX endpoint was not available through the current research interface in this stage.

Therefore:

- no claim has been made that a specific archived page was viewed when it was not;
- old product names have not been invented from domain names;
- third-party indexed records are clearly distinguished from recovered first-party pages.

If owner-held screenshots, old Shopify exports, social archives or previous catalogues become available, they would materially improve product recovery.

These clues align more naturally with a premium chocolatier/manufacturer than the fabricated-looking named products currently in the prototype.

# 12. Important false-positive controls

During research, several unrelated “Caline” results appeared.

Do **not** import:

- Patchi’s product named **Caline**;
- Bruyerre’s Belgian praline called **Caline**;
- Japanese confectionery products branded CALINE;
- Caline guitar/effects-pedal products;
- unrelated companies named Caline Chocolate outside the UAE/GCC without a clear corporate link.

Any future product candidate must have a traceable relationship to **Caline FZE / Caline Factory Boutique / the confirmed regional domains**.

---

# 13. Stage 4 conclusion

There **were older Caline websites**, but the evidence found so far points mainly to **regional commerce sites**, not one recoverable old UAE corporate website.

The most valuable historical domains are:

1. `calinechocolate-csa.com` — Saudi/Riyadh
2. `calinechocolate-om.com` — Oman
3. `calinechocolate-bh.com` — Bahrain lead

The current `calinefactory.com` domain is a 2025-era web project and should not be used to validate the old catalogue.
