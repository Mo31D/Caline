# Caline Website Audit — Stage 3: External Verification & Company Research

**Project:** Caline Chocolate  
**Repository:** `Mo31D/Caline`  
**Research date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** External fact-check of the claims inventoried in Stage 2.

## Method

This stage deliberately does **not** use the current `calinefactory.com` site as evidence for itself.

Sources were ranked roughly as:

1. official/government-zone publications and direct executive quotes;
2. reputable press;
3. historical commerce/platform records;
4. business directories/maps;
5. staff-profile corroboration.

Search results for unrelated companies/products called **Caline** were excluded. In particular, Patchi has a product named “Caline”, Bruyerre in Belgium has a praline named “Caline”, and there are other unrelated Caline businesses. Those must never be mixed into this company’s catalogue.

---

## Executive result

There is a real and independently documented **Caline Chocolate / Caline FZE manufacturing business in Hamriyah Free Zone, Sharjah**.

The strongest externally supported brand story is substantially better than the story used on the prototype:

- Caline is a chocolate manufacturer in Hamriyah Free Zone.
- The factory was publicly documented by HFZA in 2017/2018.
- In a Gulf News/HFZA feature, CEO **Abdullah Abdullatif Alarfaj** said that, **since 2016**, Caline Chocolate factory specialised in handmade Belgian chocolates.
- The same CEO statement describes a **bean-to-bar process through three factories around the world**, with the **final manufacturing process facility at Hamriyah Free Zone**.
- Historical retail/e-commerce traces exist in **Dubai/Jumeirah, Riyadh/Saudi Arabia and Oman**, with evidence also pointing to a Khobar boutique and a Bahrain e-commerce domain.
- The current website’s product names, prices, statistics, discounts and several precise company-history claims remain unsupported.

This means the new website should be built around Caline’s **real manufacturing story**, not the invented-looking catalogue currently on the site.

# 1. Manufacturing identity

## Claim: Caline is a genuine chocolate manufacturer at Hamriyah Free Zone

**Status: VERIFIED**

### Evidence

Hamriyah Free Zone Authority’s 2018 Business Zone publication contains an article titled:

> “Sweet learning experience for kids at Caline Chocolates”

It describes students visiting the **Caline Chocolates factory situated in Hamriyah Free Zone**, observing chocolate production from raw form through moulding and packaging into consumer products.

The publication names:
- **Essam Elmesiry** — Managing Director
- **Abdurrahman** — Manager of the chocolate factory

HFZA’s 2021 Business Zone publication also lists **Caline Chocolate** among the food processing and manufacturing companies operating in **Sharjah Food Park, Hamriyah Free Zone**.

### Sources

- HFZA Business Zone, Issue 45, Apr–Jun 2018  
  https://hfza.ae/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Issue-45.pdf

- HFZA Business Zone, Issue 52, 2021  
  https://hfza.ae/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Business-Zone_Issue-52_2021_Jan-June.pdf

### Website action

**KEEP and strengthen.**  
The manufacturing facility is one of the strongest authentic differentiators Caline has.

# 2. Operating history / foundation date

## Current-site claim: founded in 2009 in the UAE

**Status: UNVERIFIED / DATE TENSION**

The strongest direct executive statement found is from CEO Abdullah Abdullatif Alarfaj:

> “Since 2016, Caline Chocolate factory specialises in manufacturing the finest hand-made Belgian chocolates…”

HFZA also independently documents Caline in Hamriyah Free Zone by 2017 and 2018.

This does **not conclusively prove** that the legal business or brand did not exist before 2016, but no credible external source found in this stage supports the site’s **2009** date.

### Source

Gulf News — “YES! We did it” / HFZA business feature  
https://gulfnews.com/business/yes-we-did-it-1.1617278253127

### Website action

Do **not** publish “Founded in 2009” unless Caline can supply company-registration, trademark, historical-site or other primary documentation.

For now, the safe public wording is based on the documented factory history from **2016 onward**.

# 3. Handmade Belgian chocolate positioning

## Current-site claim: Belgian quality / Belgian chocolate

**Status: VERIFIED in principle**

The Gulf News feature directly quotes Caline Factories’ CEO describing the factory as specialising in:

> “the finest hand-made Belgian chocolates”

This is much stronger evidence than the generic “European quality” language in the current website.

### Website action

**KEEP, but use the documented formulation carefully.**

Avoid implying that every ingredient is Belgian or that the entire product is manufactured in Belgium unless Caline provides specific documentation.

---

# 4. Bean-to-bar and three-factory model

## Current-site claim: international production process

**Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED**

The CEO’s statement says Caline:

- operates a **bean-to-bar process**;
- does so through **three factories around the world**;
- operates its **final manufacturing process facility at Hamriyah Free Zone**.

This is independently publishable historical evidence.

However, the current About page goes further and specifically describes:

> Brazil farms → Belgium factory → UAE hand-packing

This exact geography and sequence was **not independently verified** in this stage.

### Website action

**KEEP the three-factory / bean-to-bar story only at the level supported by the CEO quote.**

Mark the exact Brazil/Belgium/UAE chain **VERIFY WITH OWNER** before publication.

# 22. Key conclusion for design/content strategy

The strongest authentic Caline narrative is not:

> “generic luxury chocolate store with fictional-looking gift products”

It is:

> **a Sharjah-based chocolate manufacturer with documented production at Hamriyah Free Zone, a historical handmade Belgian-chocolate/bean-to-bar positioning, and a wider regional retail footprint.**

That is materially more distinctive and credible.

The new site should therefore be designed as a **premium chocolate manufacturer + brand**, with consumer products layered on top once the real catalogue is recovered.
